# Botanical Greenhouse

A daylight glasshouse: a pale sage page that deepens toward the soil line, milk-glass cards at 80%, one leaf-green accent, a clay-amber second colour for soil and heat, and calendar heatmaps that turn watering history into a sheet of leaves.

**When to use:** Plants, irrigation, climate and garden layouts viewed in daylight.

**Light and dark:** Pinned to light with scheme-neutral values in the base theme. Sibling variants: botanical-greenhouse-night.

Apply it by copying the `tokens` object into the layout `theme` (see [[theming]]) and the `appearance` object into the layout `appearance`. Rules that still apply to it are in [[style-rules]]. Themes need Connect+ Pro on the phone; without it the default glass look is drawn.
